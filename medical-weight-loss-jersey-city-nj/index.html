    How Medical Weight Loss Works in Jersey City

    Medical weight loss treats excess weight as a chronic condition. A licensed provider reviews your history, labs and medications to find what is driving your weight, such as insulin resistance, poor sleep or a medication that raises appetite. Medication may or may not be part of it. GLP-1 medicines like semaglutide and tirzepatide are common tools because they quiet appetite and help you feel full sooner.

    Who Medical Weight Loss Is For

    Adults who have not reached a healthy weight with diet and exercise alone are the usual fit. Medication is typically an option at a BMI of 30 or above, or 27 or above with a condition tied to weight, such as sleep apnea, high cholesterol or type 2 diabetes. Coaching works below those cutoffs. Pregnancy rules out medical weight loss medication.

    Treatment Schedule in Jersey City

    Week one is the intake and review. If a GLP-1 injection is prescribed, it is taken once weekly with a dose that rises every four weeks, and if an oral medicine is chosen, it is usually taken every morning. Check-ins come every few weeks early on. Once you are stable, check-ins usually space out to every one to three months.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    The first two weeks focus on habits and, if prescribed, a starting dose. Many people on GLP-1 medicine notice less hunger within the first week or two, and mild stomach symptoms show up most after starting and after each dose increase. Expect slow, steady change. Your provider will set a realistic weekly target, often around 1 to 2 pounds.

    Side Effects in Jersey City

    Side effects depend on the treatment. Injections tend to cause stomach symptoms, and stimulant pills tend to cause dry mouth, insomnia and a quicker pulse. Fast loss raises gallstone risk. Your provider will push protein and strength training to limit muscle loss.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening covers your BMI, conditions, medications and weight history. A family history of medullary thyroid cancer, uncontrolled blood pressure, an eating disorder or pregnancy each rule out specific medications. Labs may be requested. Common asks are A1C, a lipid panel, thyroid tests and a metabolic panel from the past year.

    Medical Weight Loss Compared With Other Approaches in Jersey City

    Commercial diet programs cannot prescribe. A medical program covers the same food and habit work but adds a provider who can prescribe, order labs and change medicines that contribute to weight gain. Within medical care, options range from FDA-approved Wegovy and Zepbound to daily pills and compounded alternatives, which are not FDA-approved. Surgery remains an option for some.
