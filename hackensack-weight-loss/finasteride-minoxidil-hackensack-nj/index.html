    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Hackensack, NJ

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Hackensack

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil works on a separate track. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. Early treatment keeps the most hair, since long-bald spots seldom respond. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Hackensack

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. That early shed usually comes from minoxidil pushing resting hairs out so new growing hairs can take their place, and it typically settles by month three. By months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening at the crown. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Hackensack

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Topical minoxidil can itch or flake. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. Finasteride lowers PSA readings. Pregnant partners should not touch broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Hackensack

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
